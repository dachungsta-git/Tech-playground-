          Same job, different physics

          Triode · through a vacuum

          A heated cathode emits electrons. A wire grid limits how many reach the positive plate. Make the grid more negative to reduce current.

          nMOS transistor · through silicon

          An insulated gate creates a conducting channel. Make the gate more positive to turn this nMOS on. Drain voltage drives electrons through the channel; no heater required.
